I. INTRODUCTION

1. On 16 July 2025, the Commission presented a package of proposals on the Multiannual
       Financial Framework (MFF) for the period 2028-2034, consisting of proposals for a Council
       Regulation laying down the MFF for the years 2028-2034, for an Interinstitutional Agreement
       on budgetary discipline, cooperation in budgetary matters and on sound financial
       management, and for a Council Decision on the system of own resources of the EU. This was
       accompanied by a number of sectoral legislative proposals. On 3 September 2025, the
       Commission presented additional sectoral legislative proposals.

2. Under the Danish and Cyprus Presidencies, the Council started the examination of the MFF
       proposals in the Ad hoc Working Party on the Multiannual Financial Framework (AHWP
       MFF), its subgroups and other relevant Council preparatory bodies, reaching partial general
       approaches or partial mandates on fifteen sectoral legislative proposals.

I. HORIZONTAL

1. The next Multiannual Financial Framework (MFF) will cover the seven years between 2028
       and 2034. The budget will enable the EU to respond to current and future challenges and to
       achieve its political priorities. It covers new and established policies in an effort to ensure the
       EU’s long-term security and prosperity. Europe’s needs are many: we must take responsibility
       for our own security, including through stronger defence capabilities, protection of our
       borders and values, and migration management. We must also boost our competitiveness and
       clean transition efforts. At the same time, established policies stemming from Treaty
       obligations including economic, social and territorial cohesion and the Common Agricultural
       Policy (CAP) remain crucial to our communities and citizens. Our commitment to Ukraine is
       central to our overall ambitions, as is our continued leadership on humanitarian and
       development assistance.

2. The Multiannual Financial Framework for the period 2028 to 2034 will have the following
       structure:

       i) Heading 1: Economic, social and territorial cohesion, agriculture, rural and maritime
              prosperity and security;

       ii) Heading 2: Competitiveness, prosperity and security;

       iii) Heading 3: Global Europe;

       iv) Heading 4: Administration.

Simplification

9. The MFF will follow a simplified and streamlined structure with the aim of reducing rigidity
       and administrative burden for authorities and beneficiaries. This new structure should mitigate
       overlapping objectives between programmes and provide simplified access to funding
       opportunities and budget information for applicants and beneficiaries, while also ensuring
       coherence and promoting synergies between programmes.

10. The RAL (reste à liquider) is an inevitable by-product of multiannual programming and
       differentiated appropriations. However, the RAL is expected to reach EUR [X] billion by the
       end of the financial framework for 2021-2027, leading to payments from the current MFF
       constituting a significant amount of overall payments in the first years of the next MFF. To
       ensure a predictable level and profile, as well as an orderly progression of payments and
       increase in the responsiveness of the EU budget, several measures are taken, such as:
       simplifying implementation; setting appropriate pre-financing rates; payment and de-
       commitment rules; and timely adoption of the sectoral legislation for the MFF 2028-2034.

11. In the event of the adoption of rules or plans under shared management after 30 November
       2028, the amounts corresponding to allocations not used in 2028, except for commitments for
       prefinancing, will be transferred in equal proportions to each of the years 2029 to 2032, with a
       corresponding adjustment to the MFF ceilings.

12. Following the principle of budgetary unity, as a rule, all items of EU expenditure will be
       included in the MFF. However, given their specificities, some instruments will be placed
       outside the MFF ceilings in commitment and payment appropriations or will constitute off-
       budget items.

Flexibility

13. The Union must have the capacity to respond to exceptional circumstances and changing
       priorities, whether internal or external. At the same time, the need for flexibility must be
       weighed against predictability for long-term investments as well as the principles of
       budgetary discipline and transparency of EU expenditure, whilst also respecting the binding
       character of the MFF ceilings.

14. In line with the capacity to respond to evolving needs and reprioritising funds, the MFF
       should facilitate possible redeployments and reprogramming within and across programmes.
       Any possible deviation from the indicative financial envelope for multiannual programmes
       shall not be more than [20] % of the amount for the entire duration of the programme. This
       does not apply to appropriations pre-allocated per Member State.

15. Appropriate margins will be set within each Heading. Within certain programmes, unallocated
       amounts and cushions will be established. In an ever-evolving environment, resources for
       response to crises such as natural disasters should also be part of inbuilt flexibility within
       certain programmes without undermining their primary objectives.

II. HEADING 1 – ECONOMIC, SOCIAL AND TERRITORIAL COHESION,
       AGRICULTURE, RURAL AND MARITIME PROSPERITY AND SECURITY

27. The objective of this Heading is to deliver EU added value by fostering cohesion, upwards
       convergence, economic growth, competitiveness, resilience and security, by supporting
       investments and reforms for an inclusive, sustainable, prosperous, autonomous and secure
       Europe. Expenditure under this Heading will aim to reduce economic, social and territorial
       disparities, foster the attractiveness and development of rural areas, and support resilient,
       competitive and sustainable EU agricultural and fisheries sectors, as well as boost the blue
       economy, invest in people, reinforce the Union’s defence capabilities, bolster security and
       migration management and protect the Union’s external borders, while ensuring the good
       functioning of the single market across the Union. This heading will also cover the repayment
       of NextGenerationEU (NGEU).

28. Farming and fisheries are strategic sectors for the Union, ensuring safe, high-quality food for
       the EU and playing a key role in global food security. Stabilising farmers’ incomes, attracting
       a future generation of farmers, guaranteeing food security within the Union and supporting
       the transition towards an economically, environmentally and socially resilient, sustainable and
       market-oriented agricultural sector with a level playing field remain fundamental priorities for
       the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) financed by the EU budget. In the same spirit, the EU
       budget will continue to finance a resilient Common Fisheries Policy (CFP).

29. Promoting the overall harmonious development of the Union and reducing disparities by
       supporting a strengthening of its economic, social and territorial cohesion remain the
       cornerstone of Cohesion policy. The EU budget will continue to provide financing for
       investments and structural adjustments in all categories of regions, according to their specific
       challenges, with particular attention paid to less developed regions.

30. Coordinated action at EU level in relation to effective control of external borders is essential
       to ensure more efficient migration management and a high level of internal security in the
       Union while safeguarding the principle of free movement of persons and goods within the
       Union. The EU budget will continue to support the strengthening of internal security,
       including against security crises caused by evolving geopolitical challenges, and the
       implementation, strengthening and development of the common migration and asylum policy
       and of European integrated border management and European visa policy. Synergies with
       external policy will be ensured to address the external dimension of migration.

National and Regional Partnership Plans

38. Member States will prepare National and Regional Partnership Plans to support the general
       objectives and, taking into account the specific challenges and needs of the Member State
       concerned and its regions, to contribute to the six specific objectives and to the relevant
       specific objectives under each subparagraph of the Fund. The plans will set out reforms and
       investments and other interventions that contribute to addressing all or a significant subset of
       the relevant country-specific recommendations under the European Semester within the scope
       of the specific objectives of the Fund, the Rule of Law Report, the CAP national
       recommendations, the Digital Decade recommendations, National Energy and Climate Plans
       and relevant documents and strategies in the area of home affairs. The assessment of the plan
       will also take into account the financial allocation as well as the scope and scale of the
       country specific challenges of the Member State concerned, the principles of proportionality
       and equal treatment and whether the relevant country-specific recommendations are addressed
       in other national plans or documents adopted at the EU level.

39. The National and Regional Partnership Plans will be prepared and implemented in accordance
       with the principles of partnership and multi-level governance including national, regional and
       local authorities, and in accordance with Member States’ institutional, legal and financial
       frameworks. To this end, the design of the plan must reflect the collaboration with regional
       and other public authorities, and safeguard the role of the regions.
