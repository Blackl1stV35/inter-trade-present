# Stegra delivery-status check (policy_status/stegra)

Retrieved: 2026-10-22 (all pages opened live on that date). Scope: Stegra (formerly H2 Green Steel), Boden plant, and its Mercedes-Benz and BMW agreements, for outline slide S9 (Row 3 Limit line), S11 and A13. No numbers in this folder are estimated or recalled from memory; where a figure appears it is attributed to the source that printed it. Units: none are converted (EUR and SEK amounts are quoted as printed; the only conversion shown, SEK 10 bn "around EUR 880m", is Tech.eu's, not ours).

## Bottom line

1. Stegra's own 30 Sep 2026 release (title: "Stegra implements changes following 100-day review under new owners - Hakan Buskhe appointed CEO") says additional capital is required to complete the Boden project because completion costs are "significantly higher than assumed in June". It gives no amount. The same release also says the timeline for green steel production "remains as expected" (no date) and that "commitments to suppliers and customers remain". The workbook [U16] captures only the first part.
2. No delivery of steel to Mercedes-Benz, BMW or any other buyer, and no trial volume, is reported anywhere I could open. The Stegra newsroom (44 items, Apr 2025 to 16 Oct 2026) has no first-hydrogen, first-iron, first-steel or delivery announcement, and Stegra's own Jan 2026 release says first deliveries to thyssenkrupp are expected "to start in 2027". "None reported" is not the same as "proved none"; it is the strongest wording the evidence allows.
3. There is no published start date. Last specific company statement: production start around the turn of 2026/27 (CEO, 13 Oct 2025, via TT). Since 14 Apr 2026 the timeline is "under review"; on 18 Jun 2026 Stegra's press chief declined to give a date and said customers and closest stakeholders would get a plan "in the autumn" (not public by 22 Oct).
4. The 2025 start dates in the BMW (20 Oct 2021) and Mercedes-Benz (7 Jun 2023) announcements have passed without production. No change to either contract has been reported; contract terms are not public. No 2025-26 statement by Mercedes-Benz or BMW about Stegra was found.
5. The financing need is unresolved: CEO change takes effect 1 Nov 2026, shareholders "expressed a positive view" (30 Sep), the press (not Stegra) reports at least SEK 10 bn is needed, and Kinnevik (15 Oct) wrote Stegra down to zero and will not join the round.

## Safest wording for slide 9, Row 3, Limit line (as of 2026-10-22)

One sentence (full): "Limit: Stegra's own 30 Sep 2026 review says more capital is needed to finish the Boden plant, the 2025 start dates in the BMW (2021) and Mercedes-Benz (2023) announcements have passed, no new start date is published and no delivery to either buyer is confirmed, so these are contracts and plans, not delivered tonnes."

Slide-length version: "Limit: Stegra says more capital is needed to finish Boden; the 2025 start dates have passed and no delivery to Mercedes-Benz or BMW is confirmed."

Do not write: "delayed to 2027" as a company fact (it is press reporting and the company now says "under review"); "contract cancelled" or "customers have left" (nothing reported); "secured EUR 1.4 bn in April" (agreed in principle 14 Apr, closed 24 Jun 2026); any SEK amount for the new gap as fact.

## Checks performed

- Every dated company fact was read from the page text itself, and the date was taken from the page (dateTime or Cision timestamp), not from a search snippet.
- The 30 Sep 2026 release was cross-read in six outlets (SVT, Tech.eu, ESG Today, GMK, ArcticToday, SMM): all agree on the core statements; the amount and the "at least SEK 10 bn" figure appear only as Affarsvarlden-sourced press.
- Newsroom sweep: grep of all 44 article texts for "Mercedes" and "BMW" returned no hit; a grep for first-steel, first-delivery, start-of-production, commissioning and timeline wording returned only forward-looking statements (plus "first steel was raised" in a June 2025 construction story, which means structural steel for the building).
- Conflicts noted and not resolved in favour of the weaker source: Tech.eu says funding "secured" in April (Stegra: agreed in principle 14 Apr, closed 24 Jun); Mining Metal News (15 Apr, via Hydrogen Insight) repeats stale items ("continuous operations by late 2026", electrolysis cells missing) that conflict with Stegra's own 2 Apr and Oct 2025 releases; Stegra's undated About-us page still says "on track to produce green steel by 2026".
- Search-result summaries mentioned (a) claims on a personal blog that Porsche and Scania had left, (b) a Bloomberg item of 13 May 2026 on unlocking EUR 1.5 bn of undrawn debt, (c) an October 2025 FT insolvency report. I did not open (a) or (b) and did not see (c) first-hand; none is used for any conclusion.

## Caveats

- This is a point-in-time check on 2026-10-22; events before 16-18 Nov can change the Limit line. Re-check the Stegra newsroom, Cision, and SVT Norrbotten after 1 Nov (new CEO), after any financing announcement, and after the promised customer schedule update.
- "No delivery reported" rests on the absence of evidence from public sources; customers could have received small samples not announced. Say "not confirmed", not "none".
- Mercedes-Benz and BMW current statements could not be retrieved (blocked or gone); the BMW Group Report 2025 PDF could not be downloaded (curl returned no response), so it was not searched for "Stegra".
- Raw copies: raw/html holds the decisive pages as fetched; raw/text holds tag-stripped text of every page relied on (layout not reproduced). Large press HTML files were not kept.
