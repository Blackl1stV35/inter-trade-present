Green steel

Say goodbye to a major polluter. Our near-zero emission steel will be the greenest ore-based steel produced at scale.

Steel made differently

Conventional steelmaking uses coal in the reduction process, where iron ore is turned into iron. We replace coal with green hydrogen made using renewable electricity.​ This reduces emissions in the production process itself. In our integrated process, the main by-product is water instead of large amounts of CO₂. That is how we can reduce emissions by up to 95% compared with conventional steelmaking. For you, that means near-zero emission steel you can use seamlessly in the products you make today.

Decarbonize without redesign

Reduce embedded emissions without changing the performance, dimensions or specifications your applications rely on. Whether you make vehicles, construction systems or industrial equipment, our steel is designed to fit into existing production processes.​

Future-proof your products

Regulation, procurement standards and customer expectations are driving demand for lower embedded emissions and greater transparency. Our process is built for this shift, helping you prepare for a lower-emission market.​

Emission reduction at the source

Our CO₂ reduction happens in the production process itself, not through carbon credits or mass balancing. We replace coal with green hydrogen in the iron reduction process, enabling significant reductions in embedded CO₂ emissions.​

Modern and reliable steelmaking

Our modern, integrated steel plant is designed for low-emission production at industrial scale. For you, that means consistent quality, predictable specifications and a steel input built to scale with demand.​

Lower transition risk​

Switching to lower-emission steel should not add unnecessary complexity. We combine proven steelmaking technologies with an integrated value chain from hydrogen production to finished steel, giving you a consistent, scalable material input for the transition.​

Partner across the value chain

We work with you from specification to implementation, supporting material selection, technical requirements and integration into existing production processes. The goal is a smooth transition, built on close collaboration and a clear understanding of your needs.
