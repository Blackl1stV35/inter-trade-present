 🇸🇪 Stegra seeking further capital for 740 MW hydrogen-based steel project in Boden 
September 30, 2026
Stegra has announced that it needs further capital to complete its flagship project, a 740-megawatt (MW) hydrogen-based steel project in Boden, Sweden.
Project status and cost assessment
According to Stegra’s own report, the timetable for green steel production remains on track and the business plan remains strong. However, the assessment indicates that additional capital is required to complete the project, as its estimated costs are significantly higher than anticipated in June. The rise in costs is mainly due to significant start-up costs resulting from the prolonged suspension of work earlier this year, as well as inflation.
The accounting, project monitoring and cost management have been reviewed, and this work has largely been completed. At the same time, a robust project plan has been drawn up for the completion of the plant.
Financing and leadership
“When we took the reins, we stated that we believed the funding would be sufficient, but we could not rule out the possibility that additional funding might be required. We now have a comprehensive overview of the project on an investment-by-investment basis — showing how far we have progressed and what it has cost. The funding requirement is greater than we had hoped, but we have a solid plan to see the project through to completion, and we have identified more opportunities for outsourcing and partnerships than we had previously anticipated. Our belief in Stegra and the business opportunities following the completion of the steelworks is even stronger today than it was when we joined the project in June, and we are now working constructively with all the company’s stakeholders to find a solution to the financing requirement,” says Leif Johannsson, Chairman of Stegra’s Board of Directors.
The Board has decided to appoint Håkan Buskhe as Stegra’s new Chief Executive Officer with effect from 1 November 2026. Henrik Henriksson will step down as Chief Executive Officer and assist Håkan Buskhe with the handover of his duties. Henrik Henriksson will remain with the company in an advisory capacity, as the expertise and experience he has gained from the project are essential to ensuring its successful completion.
Shareholder dialogue
Stegra has initiated a dialogue with its largest shareholders. According to the company, they have responded positively to participating in the work to secure the capital required to complete the project. Dialogue with shareholders, financiers, partners and other stakeholders remains active.
