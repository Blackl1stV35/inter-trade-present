Reports

The Boden plant

While preparing for environmental permitting, bankability, construction and future sustainable operations, Stegra and Boden Municipality conducted multiple baseline studies and inventories of the local flora and fauna, both on the site and around the site. To utilize this data in an optimal way, we intend to publish relevant reports and knowledge that can be leveraged by others for sustainable business and societal development. For reports made by Boden, we refer to Boden Municipality.

Environment and biodiversity

Inventory of Biodiversity at Boden Industrial Park

The purpose of the Inventory of Biodiversity (field study) was to identify the natural values within the zoned industrial area, potential impacts of the construction and steel operations and to identify mitigations in relation to the natural environment. The report was conducted by Sweco and is in Swedish.

Read the report

Bird Inventory at Boden Industrial Park

The purpose of the Bird Inventory was to find and describe the bird fauna in the area of inventory. The result can be used to evaluate the impact of the construction and steel operations on the bird fauna and identification of mitigations or ecological compensation. The report was conducted by Sweco and is in Swedish.
