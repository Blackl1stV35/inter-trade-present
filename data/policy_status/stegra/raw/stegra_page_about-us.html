We are Stegra

Founded in 2020, Stegra was created with the purpose to decarbonize hard-to-abate industry. We're leading a clean industrial revolution, starting with steel.

What are hard-to-abate industries?

Abatement refers to reducing carbon emissions – while still producing the same products. Hard-to-abate industries rely heavily on fossil fuels, making emissions cuts more challenging. Those are the industries we will tackle. And we are starting with steel, to show that it is possible.

Leading the way

Our first plant is under construction in Boden in northern Sweden. We’re on track to produce green steel by 2026, using green hydrogen and green iron to slash emissions by up to 95%. But this is just the start. We want to limit the carbon footprint of other heavy industry sectors, as well. A green future is possible, and we’re making it happen.

Who we are

We are building a diverse and rapidly growing team spread across our site and offices in Boden and Stockholm. Together, we focus on delivering green impact – for our people, customers, investors, society and planet.

50/50

We strive for gender equality among our staff, including management. That is why 50 is the magic number. Currently, our management team is made up of four men and three women, and we want to do more.

55

Already about 55 different nationalities in the company coming together to change the steel industry for good!

The beginning of a new era

Our values

Head held high, eye-leveled

Head held high, eye-leveled means that we treat people with respect, and at the same time are proud of our own journey and fearlessly facing challenges. We don’t see ourselves as underdogs, but peers in all our stakeholder interactions. 

We, not me, together

It takes a village. We know that we cannot solve the climate crisis on our own – we need partnerships everywhere. We prioritize finding ways to exchange ideas and work cross-functionally where possible. We’re curious problem solvers with a can-do attitude.

Flow

We often talk about flow when things are running smoothly. Decision-making happens where it should, and we trust each other to make the best decision possible, while not slowing down.

Customer first

The demand for greener value chains has never been higher as our customers know that going green is key to becoming futureproof. To put it simply, we wouldn’t exist as a company if not for the ever-increasing demand for greener products. That’s why we’re genuinely curious about both our customers and their customers.

Our leadership guides our organization on our mission to decarbonize heavy industry – for our people, customers, investors, society and planet.
