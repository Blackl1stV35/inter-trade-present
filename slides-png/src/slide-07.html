Thailand's View (2): Linkages and Spillovers

Spillovers reach local firms through linkages and absorptive capacity. The auto sector shows how this works.

  • About 720 Tier 1 auto suppliers: roughly half foreign-owned; about 30% foreign-Thai joint ventures (GTAI)
  • Tiers 2 and 3 are mostly Thai SMEs (Alvarez and Marsal, Mar 2026)
  • Spillover channels: demonstration, labour turnover, backward and forward linkages
  • Data-center rules: two plans (Thai staff training; supply chain) before the tax exemption (Por. 3/2569, law-firm summary)
Sources: GTAI (German-language search snippet); Alvarez and Marsal, Mar 2026 (search snippet); Por. 3/2569 summaries by Tilleke and Gibbins (Mar 2026) and CTA (Aug 2026). The lecture chart figures (47% foreign majority; over 1,100 Tier 2 and 3 firms) are not verified and are not shown. Course: L3 spillovers and linkages; L1 local content.
