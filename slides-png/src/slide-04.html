Why Communities Oppose (1): Shared Resources

Data centers compete with local users for power and water, so part of their cost falls on the host community.

  • EGAT: operators requested about 3.5 GW against a system peak of about 40 GW; EGAT seeks financial safeguards for grid costs (Sept 2026)
  • Bangkok: at least 247.2 MW across operating, under-construction and planned sites; water about 2.23 million cubic metres a year (JustPow estimate)
  • Chonburi and Rayong: residents and farmers fear water shortage; at least 19 projects planned or under way (Mongabay, Mar 2026)
Sources: The Nation 40071212 and 40070626 (read live, 3 Nov 2026); Mongabay, 11 Mar 2026 (read live). Planned MW is derived from the article total. Course: L2 crowding in and out; L3 typology table, infrastructure row.
