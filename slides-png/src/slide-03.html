Why Data Centers Are a Distinct Case

Data centers are capital-heavy services with few permanent jobs, so manufacturing-based evidence may not transfer.

  • Virginia (JLARC 2024): about 74,000 jobs a year, of which about 59,000 are temporary construction and about 15,000 operations
  • About 50 operating staff per facility (same source)
  • Thailand: 521.2 billion baht from 28 data-center projects in the first half of 2025 (BOI release 92/2568)
  • Course note: manufacturing has strong linkages; services give little spillover support (Alfaro 2003)
Sources: BOI press release 92/2568 (read in full, 24 Jul 2025). Jobs split as reported in GMU STEP Brief 5 (Sept 2026), citing JLARC 2024; total 74,000 jobs and $9.1 billion GDP confirmed in the UVA Cooper Center summary of JLARC. Course: IF, Alfaro slide and notes.
