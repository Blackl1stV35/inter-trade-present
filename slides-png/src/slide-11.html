Conclusion

Data-center FDI helps Thailand when its costs are priced and its linkages are required.

  1. Test incentives against cost per job and forgone revenue (Georgia audit as the model).
  2. Make operators pay for the power and water they use (Virginia rate class and tax as models).
  3. Tie incentives to local suppliers and training, and publish the results.
Answer: data-center FDI is likely to help Thailand's current account and tax base. Its gains for workers and local firms depend on conditions that policy can set now.
