Thailand's View (1): Balance of Payments and Trade

The government sees data centers as a gain for investment and exports. The course shows the gain depends on local content.

  • BOI: 521.2 billion baht from 28 data-center projects in the first half of 2025
  • Imported equipment raises imports in the short run
  • Foreign subsidiaries that import most inputs widen the current-account deficit; local content reduces that drag
  • An export platform can raise exports (course example: Toyota Thailand)
Positive: investment inflows; tax revenue; export platform
Negative: equipment and input imports; profit repatriation
Sources: BOI release 92/2568 (read in full). Course: L1, balance of payment effect; FDI-export and FDI-import links; short and medium-term effects.
