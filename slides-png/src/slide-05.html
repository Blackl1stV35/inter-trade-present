Why Communities Oppose (2): Jobs, Information and Trust

Permanent local jobs are fewer than the investment suggests, and the information gap fuels opposition.

  • US evidence: a typical county gains about 100 to 200 jobs; large projects promise dozens to a few hundred permanent workers (Brookings, 2026)
  • Thailand: ONEP (7 Aug 2026) says data centers are not a listed EIA category; EIA applies to large or high-rise buildings, or ones in sensitive areas
  • Bangkok governor halted new data-center permits (3 Sep 2026)
  • 166 projects paused nationally while standards are drafted (5 Sep 2026)
Sources: Brookings brief (read live); The Standard, 2 Sep 2026, reporting the ONEP reply of 7 Aug 2026 (read live); The Nation 40070626 and 40070670 (read live).
