# FDI in Data Centers in Thailand: Lecture-Aligned Outline (v2)

Length: 15 minutes, 12 slides. Presentation date: 26 November 2026.
Framework: the course lectures (L4 parts 1 to 5, and "influencing factors"). Where the lectures and the earlier OLI framework differ, the lectures govern.
Fact base: fact sheet `fdi-datacenter-fact-sheet.md`. Only claims marked usable there appear on slides.

## Spine: the question and the lecture logic

Question: does FDI in data centers raise Thailand's welfare, and under what conditions?

The lectures split effects into host and home countries. For the host, they run through three transmission channels (product market, factor market, spillovers), and each effect is conditional on the type of FDI, the sector, the timeframe, and host-country conditions. The talk applies that logic to one case.
